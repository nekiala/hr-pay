# Data processing agreement

> Draft to be reviewed by a lawyer against the personal data law that applies (in the DRC, the 2023 Digital Code; in the Republic of the Congo, law no. 29-2019). Parts in square brackets are to be completed.

## 1. Roles
The Customer is the controller of its employees' data. The Provider is a processor: it processes that data only to provide HR Pay and on the Customer's documented instructions.

## 2. Data and people concerned
The Customer's employees and former employees: identity, contact details, contract and salary data, check-ins (including the GPS position at check-in), leave, evaluations, file documents, bank and mobile money accounts.

## 3. Security
- Access limited by role, and an audit log that cannot be edited.
- Encryption in transit (HTTPS) and of ID and account numbers in the database.
- A second sign-in step for sensitive roles.
- Daily backups, one copy kept off the server.

## 4. The Provider's staff
Only people who need to reach the data do, under a confidentiality commitment. Support only visits the Customer's data with the Customer's administrator's consent, read-only, and the visit is logged.

## 5. Sub-processors
Hosting: [name and country]. Sending email: [name]. WhatsApp messages: Meta Platforms. The Provider tells the Customer of any change [30] days ahead; the Customer may object.

## 6. Transfers abroad
[To be completed according to where the data is hosted and the transfer rules that apply.]

## 7. People's rights
The Provider helps the Customer answer requests for access, correction and erasure; the app exports a person's data and handles their requests.

## 8. Data breach
The Provider tells the Customer within [48] hours of becoming aware of it, with what is needed to notify the authorities and the people concerned.

## 9. End of the contract
Data is returned on request, then deleted within [90] days, unless the law requires keeping it.

## 10. Audit
The Customer may ask for the information needed to check this agreement is kept, and an on-site audit once a year, at its own cost, with [30] days' notice.
