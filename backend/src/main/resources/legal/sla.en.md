# Service levels (SaaS)

> Draft. Figures in square brackets are to be confirmed for the hosting chosen. Does not apply to an installation at the customer's.

## Availability
HR Pay is available [99.5]% of the time each month, outside announced maintenance. Availability and incidents are published on the status page.

## Maintenance
Announced at least [48] hours ahead on the status page, preferably between [10 pm and 6 am], Kinshasa time.

## Support
- Requests made from the app (Help, then Report a problem).
- Hours: [Monday to Friday, 8 am to 5 pm, Kinshasa time].
- First answer: [4] working hours when payroll or check-in is blocked, [1] working day for anything else.

## Backups
One backup a day, kept [30] days, one copy off the server. A restore test every month.

## Service credit
If a month's availability is below the commitment, the Customer gets a credit of [5]% of the monthly price for each [0.5]% missing, capped at [50]%, on request within [30] days.
