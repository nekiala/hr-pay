The Employer meets the legal obligations of medical care for the Employee and the recognised beneficiaries, completed where applicable by its medical care policy. Fitness for work: {{contract.medical}}.

The Employee protects all non-public information, in particular client data, source code, databases, architectures, contracts, prices, financial and HR information, credentials, secrets and strategic information. This obligation survives the end of the contract where its nature warrants.

## Article 19 - Intellectual property
Works created in the course of the Employee's duties or projects are governed by the law and the Employer's intellectual property agreements. The Employee may not use the intellectual assets of the Employer or its clients for personal purposes.

## Article 20 - Cybersecurity, cloud and artificial intelligence
The Employee complies with the IT charter, the cybersecurity rules and the artificial intelligence policy. In particular, disclosing passwords, keys, confidential data or source code to unauthorised services is forbidden.

## Article 21 - Exclusivity and conflicts of interest
Without prior written authorisation, the Employee may not hold a second job, freelance, consult or carry on a competing or incompatible activity. The Employee declares any conflict of interest and may not divert clients, colleagues or opportunities.

## Article 22 - Performance review
Performance is reviewed periodically against objectives, indicators, quality, deadlines, skills, collaboration, compliance with procedures and other relevant criteria.

## Article 23 - Discipline
The Employee is subject to the company rules. Any disciplinary procedure respects the law, proportionality, the right to explain and the sanctions allowed by law.

## Article 26 - Gross misconduct

## Article 27 - End of the contract

## Article 29 - Personal data

## Article 30 - Reference documents
The contract is completed in particular by the individual sheet, job description, classification sheet, pay sheet, company rules and the Employer's applicable policies, which the Employee acknowledges having received.

## Article 31 - Governing law and disputes
The contract is governed by the law of the Democratic Republic of the Congo. The parties seek an amicable settlement before, where needed, referring the matter to the competent administrative or judicial bodies.

## Article 32 - Saving clause
